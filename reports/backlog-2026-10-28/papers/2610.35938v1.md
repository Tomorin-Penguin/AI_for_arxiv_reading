## Original abstract: English key-point paraphrase / 英文摘要转述

For first-order Kerr perturbations with matter, the metric reconstruction requires radiative curvature, source-driven Ricci/trace components and non-radiative mass/angular-momentum completion. The work proves a local transport-hierarchy result under compatibility assumptions and formulates conditional global requirements, illustrating them with a thin shell and a conserved torus.

## Independent English synthesis

The construction separates radiative Weyl information, matter-driven Ricci/trace data, and non-radiative Kerr mass–spin completion. Theorem 1 establishes **local** existence and uniqueness of a triangular first-order transport hierarchy only after compatible conservation, gauge, initial-value and completion data are specified. Proposition 1 is a conditional checklist for global closure, not a global invertibility theorem. The shell is a restricted explicit benchmark; the nonspherical conserved torus exhibits the coupled Ricci channels still required for a general solution.
