## Original abstract: English key-point paraphrase / 英文摘要转述

The authors propose inequalities linking thermal entropy coefficients to universal entanglement data in three- and four-dimensional unitary conformal field theories. They present a stronger conjectural stress-tensor bound and controlled checks in weak coupling, large-N, holography and finite-N numerical examples.

## Independent English synthesis

The proposal bounds the high-temperature Cardy-growth coefficient f in 4D and 3D CFTs using respectively the Euler anomaly coefficient a and sphere free energy F. The paper combines controlled perturbative, large-N, Chern–Simons and holographic checks, plus numerical tests; these are evidence for a conjectural general inequality rather than a universal proof. For classical Einstein–scalar holography with standard energy conditions, the appendix derives an entropy bound from A''≤0 and a lower bound on h'_H.
