**English:** An interval-count approximation supplies timelike distances, anchor geometry supplies approximate Lorentz normal charts, and chart transitions estimate connection transport. Curvature is extracted from the holonomy around shrinking loops. Distinct fine and coarse scales balance accumulated edge error against the leading loop-area signal. Under fixed smooth globally hyperbolic geometry and uniform counting conditions, Theorem 4.9 proves a quantitative bound in a sample-dependent coarse Lorentz frame; Poisson results strengthen it probabilistically. The assumptions do not cover arbitrary causal posets or establish global black-hole horizons.
