## English abstract paraphrase

The authors study the factorially divergent large-frequency expansion of thermal two-point functions in holographic CFTs dual to planar or spherical AdS black holes. Bulk near-boundary equations determine the perturbative thermal-OPE coefficients, whereas exact WKB analysis associates Borel singularities with complex-radial saddle actions. Some saddles reach the black-hole singularity. In a specified complex-frequency wedge, the Borel sum of the high-energy series reconstructs the full retarded response; outside that wedge, global Stokes data and nonperturbative corrections matter.

## 中文摘要要点转述

热CFT两点函数在频率很大时有由应力张量OPE控制的渐近展开；该展开的高阶增长包含超出边界局部数据的bulk信息。作者用复径向平面的精确WKB识别Borel奇点的经典作用量，在若干动量区域能追踪到黑洞奇点；但只有特定复频率扇形内才能依靠Borel和恢复完整的retarded两点函数。

## 正文独立概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文。** 研究对象是 `CFT_d` 的 `R^{d−1}` 或 `S^{d−1}` 上的热两点函数，其对偶bulk分别为AdS_{d+1}黑膜或球形黑洞，不能直接将其二维CFT极限当作 `AdS₂/CFT₁`。作者令 `|βω|→∞` 并保持 `χ²=k²/ω²` 或球面 `χ²=ℓ(ℓ+d−2)/(R²ω²)` 不变（式2）。将热扰动部分归一化并取对数 `H=log(G_R^pert/G_R^vac)`（式13），平直边界的系数呈 `Γ(dn)` 增长，球面情形呈 `Γ(2n)`，故必须Borel化（式14–16）。

§III中的关键映射是临界Stokes路径 `l_*(bdy,a)` 的经典WKB积分 `s_{*,±}=±2∫_{l_*} λ₀`（式18）。终点 `a` 可以是复平面turning point，也可以在某些动量区是黑洞奇点本身。因此**边界高阶微扰系数的Borel奇点位置可编码bulk非局部几何**，但并非声称可以从任意有限阶或任意动量读出奇点。§IV利用Borel求和与地平线边界条件的Stokes connection，得到有严格适用区间的重建公式 `G_R/G_R^vac=exp(S_θH)`（式34）。对于平面，重建角域为 `π/2−π/d<θ<π/2+π/d`（式35）；在该域外不能省略其它非微扰数据。式(39)给出穿越临界Stokes方向时由完整量子周期决定的跳变，区别于由经典周期确定的奇点**位置**。

**English.** The distinction is between perturbative near-boundary data and the global Stokes information needed to select a retarded solution. Factorially growing high-energy thermal-OPE coefficients produce Borel singularities whose locations are classical WKB actions between the boundary and special points in the complexified bulk. Exact Borel reconstruction of the retarded correlator is conditional on the frequency chamber; beyond it, Stokes factors encode additional nonperturbative information. The (d=2) check discussed in the paper refers to CFT₂ and an AdS₃ bulk setting, not automatically to one-dimensional Schwarzian/JT dynamics.
