# 2610.35983v1 — First-order corrections to equatorial periodic orbits around rapidly rotating black holes in dynamical Chern–Simons gravity

**Authors:** Hao-Jie Lin, Tao Zhu, Jing-Fei Zhang, Xin Zhang. **Category:** gr-qc. **Source:** https://arxiv.org/html/2610.35983v1. **Batch:** 2026-10-28 authorized backlog. **Read:** 2026-10-29T07:56:44.647Z. **Sections checked:** Introduction, II.1–II.2, III.1–III.3, IV.1–IV.3, V.1–V.3, VI. **I=3/5, R=3/5:** modified-gravity orbital dynamics; transferable constrained perturbation and geometric observable methods, but not a direct AdS2/dilaton calculation.

## English abstract (independent paraphrase)
Using a rapidly rotating dynamical Chern–Simons black-hole metric at leading small coupling, the authors compute constrained first-order corrections to equatorial periodic geodesics. The matching fixes spin, specific angular momentum and rational periodicity, while allowing the energy and turning points to vary. The radial-period correction combines direct metric effects and energy compensation. They examine geometric circumferential radii, sign-changing period responses, and leading quadrupole numerical-kludge waveform phases and harmonics. A Kerr mass adjustment can mimic the leading accumulated phase correction under the specified matching prescription, but amplitude residuals can persist.

论文研究动力学 Chern–Simons 引力中快速旋转黑洞的赤道周期测地线，采用小耦合展开而不另作慢旋转展开。固定无量纲自旋、角动量与有理周期标签，能量随耦合变化以维持闭合条件。周期的一阶改变量既来自度规的直接变形，也来自为保持周期标签而进行的能量补偿。作者还比较坐标转折点和由周长定义的几何半径，并使用四极矩近似波形说明周期偏移如何积累相位。

## Model, equations and checks
- Action (§II.1, Eq.1): \(S=(16\pi)^{-1}\int\sqrt{-g}[\mathcal R-\frac12(\nabla\phi)^2+\alpha\phi R\widetilde R]\,d^4x\). Small parameter \(\zeta=\alpha^2/M^4\ll1\), metric \(g=g^{\rm Kerr}+\zeta g^{(1)}+O(\zeta^2)\).
- Periodicity (§II.2, Eq.15): \(q=|\Delta\varphi|/(2\pi)-1=w+v/z\), with \(T_{\rm cl}=zT_r\).
- **Constrained derivative (§III.2, Eqs.23–26):** \(\delta E=-q_{,\zeta}/q_{,E}\); \(\delta T_r=(\partial_\zeta T_r)_E+\delta E(\partial_E T_r)_\zeta\). This follows by differentiating \(q(E(\zeta),\zeta)=q_*\); the chain-rule step was independently checked, not the numerical integrals.
- Endpoint regularization (§III.1): map radial interval to \(r(\eta)=(r_a+r_p)/2-(r_a-r_p)\cos\eta/2\), removing apparent square-root endpoint singularities before differentiation.
- Geometric radius (§IV.2, Eq.36): \(\mathfrak R=\sqrt{g_{\varphi\varphi}}\), with variation including both metric change and turning-point displacement. The coordinate radius shift alone is not invariant.
- Numerical example (§VI): at \(a=0.99\), baseline \((3,2,1)\), the authors give \(C_T^{dir}\simeq4.7151\), \(C_T^{comp}\simeq-15.2337\), hence \(C_T\simeq-10.5186\). Arithmetic sum checked.
- Waveform (§V.2): at fixed physical time, a phase term \(-C_T u_K (H_A^K)'\) accumulates; first-order Kerr mass rescaling \((M_K-M_{ref})/M_{ref}=\zeta C_T\) can reproduce that phase effect under fixed dimensionless orbit parameters.

## Interpretation and limits
**Authors' claim:** sign and cancellation of period shifts depend on orbit family and matching prescription; frequency degeneracy with mass does not remove every amplitude difference. **Reader inference:** the fixed-observable constrained derivative is transferable to gravitational boundary-condition comparisons, where holding different observables fixed changes the linear response. **Not independently reproduced:** fitted dCS metric residuals, quadrature, parameter scans, waveform amplitudes. The test-body and numerical-kludge approximations omit self-force, radiation reaction and full gravitational radiation in dCS. The high-spin small-coupling EFT regime remains restricted.
