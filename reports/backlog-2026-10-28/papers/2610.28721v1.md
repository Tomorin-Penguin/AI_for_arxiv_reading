The authors examine a proposed black-hole ringdown damping-time stability bound in gravitational effective field theories. They argue that under ordinary one-scale power counting the would-be violation emerges only as higher-derivative corrections lose predictivity, while resolvable exceptions require unusual coefficient patterns or two-scale EFTs.

## Independent English synthesis

A proposed universal bound on EFT-induced black-hole QNM decay-time shifts is examined against signal resolvability and EFT truncation control. Classical damping-time fit uncertainties are detector and waveform dependent; under standard one-scale power counting, high-multipole shifts become order one near the regime where an infinite tower of derivative operators contributes. Thus the conjectured bound yields no new generic Wilson-coefficient constraint beyond standard amplitude positivity under the assumptions considered.
