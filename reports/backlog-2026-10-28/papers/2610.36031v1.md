## English abstract paraphrase

The authors solve the moment-recursion bootstrap for a particle on a circle by enforcing exponentially decaying Fourier moments. Minimal solutions of the recurrence admit convergent generalized continued fractions, yielding densities, wavefunctions, quasimomentum and narrow Mathieu bands.

## 独立中文正文概括

通常bootstrap把期望值条件〈HO〉=E〈O〉和正性〈O†O〉≥0转成截断moment SDP。作者对周期势将波函数概率密度的Fourier矩t_n写为有限阶递推，物理解要求n→∞快速衰减，从而选中三项递推的minimal solution。余弦势H=p²/2−2λcosθ的递推式(19)，用比值r_n=t_{n+1}/t_n转成Miller逆推的连分数(20)–(21)；Pincherle定理保证存在minimal solution时的连分数收敛。额外的密度与准动量条件选出物理能带，作者给λ=4案例的极窄能带宽约6×10^-9。**递推满足并不自动意味着任意E可物理实现**，要检查正性及normalization。

## Independent English synthesis

The construction converts the quantum-mechanical moment bootstrap into a minimal-solution problem for Fourier recurrences on a circle. Exponentially decaying moments are selected by backward continued fractions; with positivity and additional physical conditions, spectral bands and wavefunctions follow. For a cosine potential, the Mathieu spectrum includes exponentially narrow bands, which the numerical implementation resolves. Algebraic recurrence solutions alone do not guarantee a physical positive probability measure.
