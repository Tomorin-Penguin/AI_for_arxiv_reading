## 独立中英正文概括 / Independent body synthesis

**中文：** 固定电场的 Schwinger proper-time 积分含极点 $s_n=n\pi/|eE|$；形式延拓 $B\to iB_I$ 将原本的磁场双曲函数转为三角函数，又产生 $s_k=k\pi/|eB_I|$。当 $B_I/E$ 为有理数时两个极点族重合并出现高阶极点，不能直接代入非有理比的简单残数公式。作者分析无电场而有虚磁场的作用量虚部以及 Dirac/scalar 特殊符号关系；在非厄米背景中不能不加限定地把 $2\mathrm{Im}\mathcal L$ 当作正的物理产生概率率。

**English:** Analytic continuation of a constant magnetic field into an imaginary value generates an additional proper-time pole family in one-loop scalar and Dirac QED. Rational field ratios produce coincident poles requiring special residue formulae, and complex Landau spectra spoil naive lowest-level dominance. The resulting imaginary effective action has restricted probabilistic meaning for a non-Hermitian background.
