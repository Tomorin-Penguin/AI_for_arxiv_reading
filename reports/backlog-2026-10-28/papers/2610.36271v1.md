## Original abstract: English key-point paraphrase / 英文摘要转述

These pedagogical notes explain how kinematic singularities, contour pinches, unitarity cuts and multi-sheet analytic constraints restrict amplitudes and correlators. They build from Landau equations through sequential discontinuities and symbol calculus to reconstruct a simple loop integral by bootstrap.

## Independent English synthesis

The notes organize analytic information in stages: Landau pinch conditions identify potential singular loci, α-positivity restricts physical-sheet candidates, unitarity cuts alter the accessible singularities, and sequential discontinuities obey hierarchical vanishing relations. Leading singularities, symbol alphabets and boundary expansions then constrain an ansatz, illustrated by a two-dimensional massive bubble. This does not mean arbitrary correlators are fixed by their singularity locations alone.
