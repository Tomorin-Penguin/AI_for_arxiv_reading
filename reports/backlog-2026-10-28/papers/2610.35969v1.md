## 英文摘要要点转述 / English abstract paraphrase

The author refutes an observer-side periodically varying mass monopole previously inferred from static, source-position-dependent wormhole geometries. The putative variation picks different static charge sectors rather than following a single dynamical solution. Maxwell flux conservation and the first-order vacuum Einstein equations forbid the claimed time-dependent monopole, while the Bondi mass balance restricts nonlinear mass changes; genuine higher-multipole responses are not ruled out.

## 中文摘要转述

文章反驳一种假想虫洞的观测信号：当物体在另一端运动时，本端远场似乎出现周期性质量单极变化。作者指出把一族各自满足边界条件的静态解，直接当成同一个系统的时间序列，是物理错误；通过高斯定律、线性 Einstein 方程和 Bondi 能量平衡给出独立检查，同时允许随时间变化的高阶多极场。

## 独立正文阅读与推导脉络

**中文：** §II 在本端测量球面定义 $Q[S]=(4\pi)^{-1}\int_S\star F$，由 $d\star F=4\pi\star j$ 及世界管 Stokes 定理推得 $Q[S_{t_2}]-Q[S_{t_1}]=\int_{\mathcal T}\star j$，所以世界管上无电流时本端单极荷不变（(1)–(2)）。双端静态匹配的电势差 $c_1-c_2$ 是自由的；若每个源半径 $A$ 都重新强制 $c_1=c_2$，得到 $Q_2=qR/(2A)$，**其实选择了不同的 flux sector，而不是同一解的无源动力学**（(3)）。§III 对 Schwarzschild 的 $\ell=0$ 扰动 $b(t)$，$\delta G^r{}_t=2\dot b/r^2$（(5)），真空即 $\dot b=0$；§IV 对正则渐近平坦无物质辐射的本端，$\Delta E_B=-(32\pi)^{-1}\int N_{AB}N^{AB}\le0$（(6)）。这**不排除**高阶（尤其四极）响应，也不声称所有不同虫洞拓扑共享严格相同的远区条件。

**English:** This is a useful counterexample to replacing a dynamical evolution by an adiabatic *family* of static metrics. Static matching of the electromagnetic potential with equal constants at both mouths makes the observer-end monopole depend on the source position, but Gauss flux measured on a worldtube is conserved unless current traverses the tube. The corresponding gravitational monopole variation violates the vacuum $tr$ Einstein equation at linear order. For a vacuum asymptotically flat observer end, Bondi mass can decrease through radiation but cannot oscillate upward. Higher multipoles and resonant wormhole perturbations remain possible.
