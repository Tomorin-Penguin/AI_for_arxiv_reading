## English abstract paraphrase

The lecture notes survey de Sitter causal patches, representation theory and scalar QFT, canonical quantum gravity and observables in finite gravitational regions, emphasizing the role of quasi-local boundaries and observer-dependent access to cosmological information.

## 独立中文正文概括

本文科研关联最强的并非新dS/CFT公式，而是§5关于有限区域引力的良定性。对时间样边界，固定诱导度规的Dirichlet数据不是任意可选：Gauss–Codazzi约束(5.4)及线性化(5.5)限制边界扰动。欧氏情形泛Dirichlet问题不满足椭圆性，但特殊背景/数据可能例外。改为固定边界诱导度规的共形类及外挠率迹K（式5.7）时，欧氏边界问题在所引条件下可成椭圆；Lorentzian良定性**仍为猜想/受限线性分析**。§5还比较de Sitter worldtube和stretched horizon、关系型观测量和守恒；§2–4提供dS causal patch、SO(1,2)/SO(1,4)表示与canonical WdW框架。不要把dS正Λ边界直接等同AdS₂渐近边界。

## Independent English synthesis

The most transferable part is the analysis of observer-centered finite-region gravity. Dirichlet data at a finite timelike boundary are constrained and need not define a generic well-posed problem. Fixing the conformal class of the induced metric together with the mean extrinsic curvature gives an elliptic Euclidean setup in cited results; the analogous Lorentzian problem remains only conjecturally well-posed. de Sitter static patches, quantum representations and Wheeler–DeWitt material frame the broader context rather than proving an AdS2 boundary observable.
