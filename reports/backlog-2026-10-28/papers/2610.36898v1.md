## Independent English synthesis

These lecture notes derive Schwinger–Keldysh open EFTs from normalization, Hermiticity and positivity constraints, separating causal response from stochastic noise. They extend the operator analysis to dissipative inflationary fluctuations and discuss the unresolved challenges of gauge and gravitational open systems. A proposed set of Stückelberg fields represents fluid clocks and rulers; the construction is an outlook, **not** an established relational-clock observable in JT gravity.
