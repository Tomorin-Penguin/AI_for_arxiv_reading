## English abstract — faithful paraphrase

The authors introduce QFT-Bench, 75 graduate/research-level closed-form problems covering eight QFT subfields. They evaluate 39 models using symbolic equivalence checks with an LLM judge as fallback. Frontier systems achieve high final-answer accuracy, but may still make normalization, convention and reasoning errors. Correct final answers do not always follow from the written derivation.

## Method and key quantitative results (authors' claims)

1. **Dataset:** 75 problems: classical 7, quantization 8, QED 7, renormalization 6, non-Abelian/QCD 6, electroweak 8, SUSY 18, CFT 15 (Table 2). Problem statements are revised to specify conventions, assumptions and target observables.
2. **Verification:** compare boxed result against reference via SymPy; if symbolic matching is inconclusive, Opus-5 assesses mathematical/physical equivalence (§3.3). Thus reported accuracy is *not* exclusively a theorem-prover-verified rate.
3. **Results:** Table 4 reports GPT-6.1-Sol 100.0% average, Opus-5.5 98.7%, and substantial variation across models; twelve models exceed 95% (§5.1). These are benchmark results, not guarantees for open-ended research.
4. **Faithfulness metric:** §5.3 defines \(u_m=U_m/C_m\), where \(C_m\) counts correct final answers and \(U_m\) counts correct answers whose displayed reasoning is judged unsupported. The paper reports 7.2% mean unfaithfulness across 39 models (three-run averaging); the metric relies on another LLM judge.
5. **Concrete logical inconsistency:** Appendix/example in §5.3 points out \(\lambda^2+(\lambda-1)^2-2\lambda(\lambda-1)=1\), whereas a model asserted a parameter-dependent central charge from this numerator. This algebraic identity is independently checked by expansion; it illustrates a correct-looking conclusion not justified by the shown step.

## Interpretation and limitations

**Authors' conclusion:** strong progress in QFT calculation benchmarks, but reasoning faithfulness remains a concern. **Reader's inference:** for AI-assisted gravity calculations, separately audit assumptions, gauge/sign conventions, intermediate identities and boundary terms; a final correct formula does not validate an inconsistent derivation. **Not established:** extrapolation from 75 closed-answer questions to novel theorem discovery or trustworthy independent physics research. Small topic-specific counts (e.g. six renormalization questions) and the LLM fallback grader limit statistical/verification strength.

**Research transfer:** a useful *evaluation protocol*, not a new gravitational result: prepare self-contained, convention-fixed problems and independently check derivation identities before trusting AI-generated holographic or covariant phase-space manipulations. No complete benchmark replication was performed.
