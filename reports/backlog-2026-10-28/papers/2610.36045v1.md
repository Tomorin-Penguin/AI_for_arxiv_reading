## English abstract paraphrase

For specific massless theories in odd-dimensional de Sitter spacetime, the tree-level modulus squared of the late-time wavefunctional is local. Nonlocal wavefunction information can remain in the phase, while local logarithmic divergences of corresponding Euclidean AdS actions determine the measure.

## Independent English synthesis

At tree level in selected massless theories on odd-dimensional de Sitter backgrounds, the late-time probability measure |Ψ|² is local, as can be traced to local logarithmic divergences in a corresponding EAdS on-shell action. The full wavefunctional retains nonlocal phases, and mixed field–momentum observables can detect them. The parity of spacetime dimension, field content and tree approximation are essential restrictions.
