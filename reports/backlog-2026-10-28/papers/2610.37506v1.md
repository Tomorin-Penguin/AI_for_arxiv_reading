## Independent English synthesis

The trace-anomaly-induced action alone gives a subleading r^{-4} potential correction in this Schwarzschild far-field setup. Adding a Visser-parameterized vacuum-polarization stress tensor in the Boulware state recovers the expected mass-linear r^{-3} scaling. However, the coefficient is an adjustable ansatz parameter fixed by matching an independent one-loop result, not a first-principles prediction from the trace anomaly.
