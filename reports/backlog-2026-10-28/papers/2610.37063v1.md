## English abstract paraphrase

A minimally modified gravity model is used to construct a Genesis cosmology asymptoting to Minkowski spacetime in the past. The background is past geodesically complete with ordinary NEC-satisfying scalar matter, while perturbation stability and cubic interaction scales are analyzed conditionally.

## 独立中文正文概括

文章展示‘必须违反NEC才可能Genesis’依赖GR背景方程。VCDM型引力的constraint structure改变了H与普通标量场NEC的联系（结论5.1），以a→常量、H→0的过去Minkowski得到背景时空及tensor模的过去测地完备性。标量扰动运动学存在动量相关系数，某一有限红外区间c_R²<0，却不意味着UV ghost或所有非零k都不稳定；完整canonical frequency还含z''/z。§3.4特别警告有动量相关有效系数时无法为所有scalar模构造单一局域effective metric。§4证明在固定非零动量和渐近过去条件下，背景依赖三次耦合消退，剩余Planck抑制项；**并非所有软动量、所有阶数都无强耦合**。

## Independent English synthesis

The VCDM constraint structure permits a past-asymptotically Minkowski Genesis phase with canonical matter satisfying the NEC. The metric/tensor sector is past geodesically complete. A transient negative scalar sound-speed squared in an infrared band is not a UV gradient instability, and momentum-dependent scalar kinetic coefficients obstruct interpreting all modes via one local effective metric. Cubic background-dependent couplings vanish for fixed nonzero momenta toward the far past, with Planck-suppressed residual interactions; this does not establish arbitrary-order strong-coupling control.
