## English abstract paraphrase

For interacting scalar theories on M4×S1, tracing out heavy Kaluza–Klein modes produces a mixed state of accessible zero modes even when low-energy scattering amplitudes decouple. Perturbative channel-dependent mutual information and UV tails quantify this mismatch between local effective dynamics and reduced quantum states.

## Independent English synthesis

Decoupling of low-energy amplitudes does not imply factorization of quantum states after KK reduction. A free-field vacuum factorizes across levels, while cubic and quartic interactions entangle accessible zero modes with the heavy tower at order λ². Cubic level-wise mutual information can have a 1/m_n tail and a logarithmic sum, whereas additional hard momenta in quartic channels change the cutoff behavior. A local EFT for amplitudes alone need not retain this information without a reduced-state prescription.
