## Independent English synthesis

Classical radiation reaction is obtained from dimensionally regularized worldline self-fields for an electromagnetically charged spinning body, through quadratic spin order. The dissipative correction to its electromagnetic scattering waveform agrees with an on-shell one-loop Compton-cut construction. This is a bridge between retarded classical reaction and amplitude unitarity cuts; it does not by itself calculate gravitational memory or gravitational Kerr self-force.
