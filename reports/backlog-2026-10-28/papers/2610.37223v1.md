## 独立中英正文概括 / Independent body synthesis

**中文：** 在 Einstein–O(3) sigma 模型中，标量三重态满足 $\phi^a\phi^a=1$，势 $U=\mu^2(1-\phi^3)-\beta(1-\phi^3)^4$ 提供额外 scalaron 分支。旋转场相位 $n\varphi-\omega t$ 在 Killing 视界上满足同步条件 $\omega=n\Omega_H$。作者数值发现真空 Kerr 连接型和 scalaron 支持型两类有毛黑洞；后者可显著改变局部视界几何而保持相近的 ADM 总质量和总角动量。$M=M_H+M_\phi$、$J=J_H+J_\phi$ 及 Smarr $M=2T_HS+2\Omega_HJ_H+M_\phi$ 突出外部场贡献。

**English:** A constrained Einstein–O(3) sigma model with a potential allowing a scalaron sector exhibits two distinct numerical families of stationary hairy black holes. Synchronization of the rotating scalar phase removes horizon flux. The second family can have markedly non-Kerr horizon geometry even when global ADM charges are similar. The mass and angular-momentum decompositions and Smarr relation track exterior-field contributions, not a uniqueness theorem.
