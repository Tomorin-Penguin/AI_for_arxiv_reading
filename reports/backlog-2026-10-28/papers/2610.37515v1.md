## English abstract paraphrase

A unified stationary axisymmetric method reconstructs Kerr deformations from curvature and physical sources. At leading order in six higher-curvature couplings, the authors exhibit closed analytic solutions for arbitrary subextremal spin and compare horizon and observational properties.

## Independent English synthesis

The paper reconstructs stationary axisymmetric higher-curvature deformations of Kerr by combining a sourced Teukolsky curvature solution with Ricci-driven metric quadratures. For six interactions it obtains leading-coupling closed forms retaining generic subextremal spin dependence. The result is neither fully nonlinear beyond-Einstein gravity nor a generic time-dependent collapse solution; extremal and source-boundary subtleties remain model dependent.
