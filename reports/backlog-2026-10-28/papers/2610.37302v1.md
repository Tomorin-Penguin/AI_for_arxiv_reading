## 独立正文阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 文章重点不是对某个背景直接算出 Brown–Henneaux 中心荷，而是把“荷是否可积、该用哪一个 Hamiltonian、中心 2-cocycle 来自哪里”统一编码到 Noetherian split 中。基本规范关系写成 $-I_\xi\Omega\hat{=}\delta Q_\xi-F_\xi$（式 11）。对零通量，$Q_\xi$ 本身是生成元，式 (16)–(17) 给出中心项；如果 $F_\xi=\delta P_\xi$，则应先改用 $H_\xi=Q_\xi-P_\xi$，式 (51)–(52) 的 cocycle 才是 Hamiltonian 荷代数的中心项。§3 将这一处理用于具有 PBH 类型边界对称的 AdS₃：式 (142)–(144) 明确出现可积 Weyl flux，收紧至 Brown–Henneaux 边界条件时退化为零通量情形。此结论依赖作者采用的“ambiguity-free”势构造、给定边界变分与可积性条件，**不能直接等同于所有协变相空间处方的普遍唯一性**。

**English:** The paper isolates a structural question: how to obtain a canonical charge algebra and its central cocycle once field-dependent diffeomorphisms and boundary flux are present. The identity $-I_\xi\Omega\hat{=}\delta Q_\xi-F_\xi$ separates the Noether charge from the flux. With zero flux, the Noether charge generates the symmetry; with exact field-space flux $F_\xi=\delta P_\xi$, the Hamiltonian generator must be shifted to $H_\xi=Q_\xi-P_\xi$, modifying the central extension. The AdS3/PBH example exhibits a nonzero but integrable Weyl flux and reduces to the zero-flux Brown–Henneaux case under stronger boundary conditions. The conclusions are conditional on the authors' ambiguity-free prescription and chosen boundary conditions, not a proof that all phase-space prescriptions are identical.
