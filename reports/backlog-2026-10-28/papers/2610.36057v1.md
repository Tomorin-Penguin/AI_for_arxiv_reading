## English abstract paraphrase

For low-lying 1/4-BPS primaries of the Sym^N(T^4) D1–D5 orbifold CFT, second-order marginal conformal perturbation theory gives lifting matrices exact in N. Some short multiplets recombine, while a finite set survives, consistent in these sectors with the supergravity-point BPS count.

## Independent English synthesis

By evaluating twist-sector OPE coefficients and mixing matrices at order λ² around Sym^N(T⁴), the authors obtain anomalous dimensions exact in N for selected low-lying 1/4-BPS sectors. Recombination lifts all states in the two lowest selected right-spin sectors, but ten primaries survive for right spin one (N≥3), matching a specified supergravity-point count. The result is restricted to selected quantum numbers and perturbative order.
