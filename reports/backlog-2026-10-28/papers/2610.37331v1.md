## Independent English synthesis

For static neutral and charged T-duality-inspired regular metrics, the authors classify horizons, circular orbits, tidal curvature eigenvalues measured by infalling observers and numerical geodesic deviation. The regularization removes the central curvature divergence, and photon-orbit features can persist into horizonless parameter regimes. Finite tidal forces and their accumulated effects are distinct from any proof of a global collapse spacetime or a full string-theoretic solution.
