## Independent English synthesis

A numerical-relativity signal from a pair of initially horizonless solitonic boson stars is analyzed with standard Kerr-vacuum-GR inspiral and ringdown models. The inferred area increase can have posterior support approaching 92% despite the absence of initial horizons. The merger **does form a black hole**, and this result diagnoses model dependence of the test, not a violation of Hawking's area theorem.
