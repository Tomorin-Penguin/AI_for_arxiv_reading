## English abstract paraphrase

For one-scale integrable sigma-model families satisfying the Courant–Hilbert condition, exact irrelevant flows can be expressed using stress-tensor invariants. The analysis includes generalized Born–Infeld, power-law, logarithmic families, dimensional extensions and a commuting root-type deformation.

## 独立中文正文概括

作者不是声称任意二维QFT都能被某一个普适TbarT流描述。关键先验是sigma模型的拉氏量满足Courant–Hilbert方程及一尺度假设；通过将应力张量不变量X=T_{μν}T^{μν}与Y=(T^μ_μ)²写成同一变量，消去参数得到∂_λ L = X F(√(Y/X))的自治流，F依赖模型的CH函数。§§4–7列出GBI、q、log等族及临界电流时的分支奇点；§8推广任意维，仍有维数与特定谱假设约束；§9的commutativity是该受控CH族中的root-TbarT流对易结果，**不是全部变形算符彼此对易**。

## Independent English synthesis

The exact stress-tensor flows arise within constrained one-scale Courant–Hilbert integrable sigma-model families. Their autonomous functions depend on the underlying model, while specified root-type deformations commute and preserve classical integrability. Physical domains exclude critical-current branch singularities; the claimed extension to arbitrary dimension carries its own stress-eigenvalue assumptions. The results are not a universal flow for every two-dimensional QFT.
