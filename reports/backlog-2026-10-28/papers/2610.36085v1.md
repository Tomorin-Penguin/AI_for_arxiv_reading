## English abstract paraphrase

Static Einstein–Maxwell–scalar black-hole branches can respond nonlinearly to an imposed external scalar field. The sign and magnitude of that source change scalar charge, the range of allowed electric charge and the multiplicity of equilibria, with implications for companion-induced fields in binary configurations.

## 独立中文正文概括

这里关键是区分标量场的外源/渐近值与黑洞响应荷。固定质量、静态电荷及外部标量源，并非总能对应唯一解：正标量源可在零源自发scalarization阈值以下感应正荷；负外源存在有限下界，且某些相同源/熵/电荷组合有不同荷和质量的分支。§III 的二体平衡以两孤立天体标量长程场的领先近似相互作用代替完整非线性双黑洞模拟，同号荷不必随间距单调增大，异号荷可有多支平衡。在§IV及附录强调作者数值分支/响应量，不是证明所有二体解的稳定性。

## Independent English synthesis

The external scalar boundary value acts as a source distinct from the black-hole scalar charge. The stationary Einstein–Maxwell–scalar response is non-monotonic and can be multivalued, with positive sources inducing hair below the spontaneous-scalarization threshold and negative sources restricting solution domains. Binary mutual induction is treated via leading companion fields, not a fully evolved nonlinear binary spacetime.
