## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** WKB–Padé 与时域演化交叉检查了频率，但晕边缘平滑方式会明显影响阻尼。主模型有径向张力且远处质量对数增长；不能直接当作现实有限质量晕中的引力波预测。

**English:** WKB–Padé and time-domain calculations cross-check frequencies, while edge smoothing affects damping. The main halo model has radial tension and logarithmically growing asymptotic mass; these probe spectra are not gravitational-wave predictions for a realistic finite-mass halo.
