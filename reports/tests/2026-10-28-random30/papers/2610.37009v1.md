Momentum-space entanglement and mutual information in Kaluza-Klein reduction

## 作者英文摘要

In theories with compact extra dimensions, the four-dimensional effective description is obtained by Kaluza-Klein reduction. The state available to a four-dimensional observer is the reduced density matrix obtained by tracing the higher-dimensional vacuum over the tower of massive modes, and it need not be pure. In this paper we study the momentum-space entanglement of this state for a five-dimensional scalar on $M_4\times S^1$ with a cubic or quartic self-interaction. We compute, at leading order in the coupling, the mutual information $I_n$ between the zero modes below a resolution scale $\mu$ and a single Kaluza-Klein level $n$. When the zero modes couple linearly and without derivatives to pairs of tower modes, $I_n$ falls as $1/m_n$, where $m_n$ is the mass of level $n$. In the cubic theory, this tail is independent of the ultraviolet cutoff, with coefficient $(\mu R)^2/1024\pi^3$ for compactification radius $R$, and the mutual information with the whole tower grows logarithmically with the number of levels. In the quartic theory, the tail is masked by states containing additional hard quanta, which make $I_n$ depend on the cutoff. Compared with the corrections the tower makes to low-energy amplitudes, which are dominated by the lightest levels and captured by local operators, the mutual information with the tower is not dominated by any finite set of levels and arises from the time-nonlocal part of the effective action. The local four-dimensional effective theory therefore describes the low-energy observables of the reduced system but not its state. Unbroken supersymmetry cancels the vacuum energy of the compactification but not the mutual information, which at leading order is a sum of squared amplitudes.

## 摘要中文翻译

在具有紧致额外维度的理论中，四维有效描述由 Kaluza–Klein 约化得到。四维观测者可获得的态，是对高维真空中的整座重模塔取偏迹得到的约化密度矩阵，它不一定是纯态。本文研究 M₄×S¹ 上具有三次或四次自相互作用的五维标量场中，这种态的动量空间纠缠。我们在耦合常数的领阶计算分辨率尺度 μ 以下的零模与单个第 n 层 Kaluza–Klein 模之间的互信息 Iₙ。当零模以线性且不含导数的方式耦合到成对的模塔模式时，Iₙ 随 1/mₙ 衰减，其中 mₙ 是第 n 层的质量。三次理论中，这一尾部与紫外截断无关，其系数为 (μR)²/(1024π³)，R 为紧致化半径；与整座模塔的互信息随层数呈对数增长。四次理论中，包含额外硬量子的态掩盖了这一尾部，使 Iₙ 依赖截断。模塔对低能振幅的修正由最低的几层主导，并可由局域算符刻画；与之相比，零模与模塔的互信息不由任何有限组层主导，而来自有效作用量中时间非局域的部分。因此，局域四维有效理论描述约化系统的低能可观测量，却不描述其完整的态。未破缺超对称性消除紧致化的真空能，但不消除互信息，因为后者在领阶是振幅平方之和。

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 五维圆紧致化标量模型中，非导数三次耦合给出单层互信息的 1/n 尾，整座模塔贡献对层数呈对数增长。四次耦合另受硬动量态影响。这里区分的是低能可观测量与约化密度矩阵；并非宣称有效场论失效。

**English:** For a scalar on a circle, nonderivative cubic interactions produce a 1/n mutual-information tail and logarithmic tower accumulation; quartic interactions introduce additional cutoff sensitivity. The distinction concerns low-energy observables versus the complete reduced density matrix, not a failure of effective field theory for its usual observables.
