## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** Stiefel 谐波与角动量联合标签把相空间割积分化为通道重叠。正文据此组织 SMEFT 非重整化规则。结论依赖无质量、未破缺理论及指定微扰阶数；一般高圈红外扣除仍需处理。

**English:** Joint Stiefel-harmonic and angular-momentum labels turn cut integrals into channel overlaps, organizing SMEFT mixing restrictions. The setup is massless and unbroken, at specified perturbative order; a general treatment of higher-loop infrared subtraction remains open.
