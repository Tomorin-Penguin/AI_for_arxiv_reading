## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** TOV 型方程连接不同 f(Q) 模型与质量—半径关系。部分参数能抬高最大质量，却同时给出过大半径；高质量本身不意味着优于 GR。平衡序列判据也不替代完整扰动稳定性分析。

**English:** TOV-like equations relate f(Q) models to mass-radius sequences. Raising the maximum mass can simultaneously produce excessive radii, so a high mass alone is not an improvement over GR; equilibrium criteria do not replace full perturbative stability.
