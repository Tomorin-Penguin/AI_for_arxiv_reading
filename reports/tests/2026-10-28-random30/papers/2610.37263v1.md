## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 自由外态与守恒流消去冗余协变量，非最小矢量的纵向项不新增物理振幅。核光学势中超出线性阶的差异来自嵌入方案，不能直接解释为新的基本相互作用。

**English:** Free external states and current conservation remove redundant covariants; the longitudinal nonminimal vector adds no physical amplitude. Differences beyond linear order in optical-potential embeddings concern the embedding prescription, not new elementary interactions.
