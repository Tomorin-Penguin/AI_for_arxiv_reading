## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 协变测度处方给出 f₂=0（AdS₅×S⁵）及 3/(32π²)（AdS₄×CP³）；后者可由 M2 高 KK 模抵消。IIB 一侧的缺失贡献仍未解释。这里的 AdS₂ 是弦世界面，不是 JT 时空。

**English:** The covariant measure prescription gives f₂=0 in AdS₅×S⁵ and 3/(32π²) in AdS₄×CP³. Higher M2 KK modes cancel the latter; the missing IIB contribution remains unresolved. AdS₂ here is a string worldsheet, not JT spacetime.
