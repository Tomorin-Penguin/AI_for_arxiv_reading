## 作者英文摘要

We study the role of the Hull connection in the heterotic superpotential for N=1 d=4 vacua. The integrand is defined in terms of H, which is in turn defined in terms of a gravitational Chern--Simons coupling involving H. We show this recursive structure is key to W reproducing some of the ten--dimensional supersymmetry conditions written down by Bergshoeff--de Roo through \alpha'^3. With this evidence in hand we make some observations about extending some of the tensor equations of Hull and Strominger to all orders in \alpha'.

## 摘要中文翻译

我们研究 Hull 联络在 N=1、d=4 真空的杂化弦超势中的作用。被积式以 H 定义，而 H 本身又通过一个包含 H 的引力 Chern–Simons 耦合定义。我们表明，这种递归结构是超势 W 重现 Bergshoeff–de Roo 写出的部分十维超对称条件、直至 α′³ 阶的关键。在此证据基础上，我们对把 Hull 与 Strominger 的部分张量方程延伸到 α′ 的全阶提出若干观察。

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 不能把 H 内的引力 Chern–Simons 联络当作独立量冻结。递归变分使部分 F 项与十维超对称条件匹配至 α′³；比较使用低阶 D 项条件，全阶表达目前仍属推断。

**English:** The gravitational Chern–Simons connection inside H must be varied recursively. This matches selected F-term equations to ten-dimensional supersymmetry through α′³ using lower-order D-term conditions; proposed all-order expressions remain conjectural.
