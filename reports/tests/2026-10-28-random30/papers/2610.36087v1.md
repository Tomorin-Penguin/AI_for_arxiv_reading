A gaussian-process reconstruction of gravitational-wave friction from dark standard sirens

## 作者英文摘要

Standard sirens provide a direct probe of the cosmic distance-redshift relation and therefore a powerful test of whether gravitational waves (GWs) propagate as predicted by general relativity on cosmological scales. In many extensions of general relativity, GW propagation is modified by an anomalous friction term, which alters the amplitude evolution and leads to a GW luminosity distance that differs from its electromagnetic counterpart. In this work, we develop a non-parametric reconstruction of this anomalous friction from dark standard sirens. We reconstruct its redshift dependence, encoded in the friction function $\alpha_M(z)$, with a Gaussian process (GP) and derive the corresponding GW-to-EM luminosity-distance ratio $\Xi(z)$ by integration, explicitly retaining the relation between the local friction modification and its accumulated effect on the observed distance, while enforcing a monotonic and invertible GW luminosity distance. We apply the method to the GWTC-5.0 catalog, jointly inferring the source population, modified propagation, and background cosmological parameters. We find no evidence for departures from GR, with $\alpha_M=0$ and $\Xi=1$ consistent with the posterior over the redshift range probed by the catalog. The result is stable under variations of the GP kernel, smoothness and amplitude priors, resolution prescription, and likelihood treatment. We further introduce function-space diagnostics for comparing flexible and parametric reconstructions, including prior calibration, prior-to-posterior contraction, common summaries of propagation-population degeneracies, and principal-component analysis. These tools make explicit which features are informed by the data and which are imposed by the function-space prior, providing a framework for interpretable non-parametric tests of gravitational-wave propagation.

## 摘要中文翻译

标准汽笛直接探测宇宙距离—红移关系，因此可有力检验宇宙学尺度上的引力波传播是否符合广义相对论。许多广义相对论的扩展通过异常摩擦项修正引力波传播，改变振幅演化，使引力波光度距离不同于电磁光度距离。本文利用暗标准汽笛，对这一异常摩擦进行非参数重建。我们用高斯过程重建编码于摩擦函数 α_M(z) 中的红移依赖，再通过积分得到引力波与电磁光度距离之比 Ξ(z)，显式保留局域摩擦修正及其对观测距离的累积效应之间的关系，同时要求引力波光度距离单调且可逆。我们将方法用于 GWTC-5.0 目录，联合推断源种群、传播修正及背景宇宙学参数。在目录探测的红移范围内，没有发现偏离 GR 的证据：α_M=0 和 Ξ=1 与后验一致。改变高斯过程核、光滑性与振幅先验、分辨率处方及似然处理方式后，结果保持稳定。我们进一步引入函数空间诊断，以比较灵活重建与参数化重建，包括先验校准、先验到后验的收缩、传播—种群退化的共同概括以及主成分分析。这些工具明确区分哪些特征由数据提供信息、哪些由函数空间先验施加，从而为可解释的引力波传播非参数检验提供框架。

**English:** The method reconstructs α_M(z), integrates it into Ξ(z), and enforces an invertible distance relation. GWTC-5.0 is consistent with GR. Prior and population degeneracies mean robust accumulated effects do not establish every local feature of the friction function.
