## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 由淬火平均、重叠矩阵到一步副本对称性破缺，提供进入无序多体计算的路线。全息部分回顾的 quiver 模型并不具有真正的自旋玻璃相，不能据类比认定 AdS₂ 碎裂就是玻璃化。

**English:** Quenched averaging, overlap matrices and one-step replica breaking provide a route into disordered many-body calculations. The discussed quiver models do not possess a genuine spin-glass phase, so the holographic motivation is not an identification of AdS₂ fragmentation with glassiness.
