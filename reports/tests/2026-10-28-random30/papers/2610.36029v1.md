## 作者英文摘要

We present AlgebrasTQD, an open-source code written in GAP (Groups, Algorithms, and Programming), for computing condensable algebras and the corresponding topological boundaries and interfaces of twisted quantum doubles $D^\omega(G)$. These algebras can be used to classify 1+1d gapped and gapless phases whose symmetry is related by gauging to a finite group $G$ with anomaly $\omega\in H^3(G,U(1))$, to study 2+1d anyon proliferation and to construct logical gates in non-Abelian topological quantum codes. The functions in AlgebrasTQD compute all condensable algebras $A(H,F,\gamma,\epsilon)$ with their algebra structure and anyons, sort them into isomorphism classes and identify the twin algebras that are inequivalent but share the same anyon decomposition. AlgebrasTQD also determines all the subalgebra relations, the corresponding Hasse diagram and the types of 1+1d phases for any chosen, possibly non-invertible, group-theoretical symmetry. For non-maximal algebras, the code computes the reduced topological order with its $3$-cocycle and the map of anyons across the topological interface. Finally, AlgebrasTQD includes functions that construct certain non-Clifford logical gates via non-Abelian surface codes. We summarize the formulas that are implemented in the AlgebrasTQD code, and we illustrate its use with Abelian and non-Abelian groups including $\mathbb{Z}_2^3$ with $\omega_\text{III}$, $D_8$, $A_4$ with and without anomaly, twin algebras and twin phases, and the Clifford-hierarchy gates in quantum doubles of dihedral groups. All arithmetic is exact and AlgebrasTQD can be used with any finite group $G$ and 3-cocycle $\omega$, limited only by the time and size of the calculation. The code is available at https://github.com/AlisonWarman/AlgebrasTQD

## 摘要中文翻译

我们介绍 AlgebrasTQD：一个用 GAP（Groups, Algorithms, and Programming）编写的开源程序，用于计算扭曲量子双 D^ω(G) 的可凝聚代数，以及相应的拓扑边界与界面。这些代数可用于分类 1+1 维有隙及无隙相，其对称性可通过规范化与具有反常 ω∈H³(G,U(1)) 的有限群 G 相关联；也可研究 2+1 维任意子增殖，以及构造非阿贝尔拓扑量子码的逻辑门。AlgebrasTQD 的函数计算全部可凝聚代数 A(H,F,γ,ε) 的代数结构和任意子内容，将其划分为同构类，并识别不等价但具有相同任意子分解的孪生代数。程序还确定所有子代数关系、相应的 Hasse 图，以及任意选定的、可能不可逆的群论型对称性下的 1+1 维相类型。对于非极大代数，程序计算约化拓扑序及其 3-余循环，并计算任意子跨越拓扑界面的映射。最后，AlgebrasTQD 包含利用非阿贝尔表面码构造某些非 Clifford 逻辑门的函数。我们总结程序实现的公式，并以阿贝尔及非阿贝尔群展示其用法，包括带 ω_III 的 Z₂³、D₈、具有或不具有反常的 A₄、孪生代数与孪生相，以及二面体群量子双中的 Clifford 层级门。全部运算采用精确算术；只要计算时间与规模允许，AlgebrasTQD 可用于任意有限群 G 和 3-余循环 ω。程序地址见作者摘要所列仓库。

**English:** The package retains multiplication data, distinguishing inequivalent twin algebras with identical anyon decompositions. Cochain conditions become linear congruences modulo one.
