## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 同步条件 ω=nΩ_h 保证视界处无净标量通量。负势区域允许连接真空的 type I 与标量子支持的 type II 分支；两者几何不同。数值存在性尚不保证动力学稳定。

**English:** Synchronization ω=nΩ_h removes net scalar flux at the horizon. Negative potential regions permit vacuum-connected type-I and scalaron-supported type-II branches with different geometry. Numerical existence does not establish dynamical stability.
