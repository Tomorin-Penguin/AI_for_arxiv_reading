## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 阶乘增长的高频展开经 Borel 变换连接到 WKB 作用量。完整推迟关联函数只在指定复频楔形区由此重建；区外需要全局 Stokes 数据，不能从局部展开直接宣称重建整个黑洞内部。

**English:** Factorially growing high-frequency coefficients encode WKB actions through Borel singularities. Exact retarded reconstruction holds in a specified complex-frequency wedge; global Stokes data are needed outside it. This does not by itself reconstruct the entire interior.
