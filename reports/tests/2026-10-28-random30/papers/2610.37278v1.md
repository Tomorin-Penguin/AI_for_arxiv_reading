## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 保留 lapse 的约束体系区分三类势坐标分支，零型分支可显式积分。BF 项驱动形式场间能量交换，本身不提供负压。指数模型的稳定性分析不能无条件套用于所有 Noether 分支。

**English:** A lapse-preserving reduction separates three branches and explicitly integrates the null branch. The BF term mediates energy exchange rather than supplying negative pressure itself. Stability established for an exponential model must not be transferred automatically to every branch.
