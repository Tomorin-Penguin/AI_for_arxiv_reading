## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 热准备、规范协变紫外滤波及质量淬火产生带电磁单极子，随后显著失去电荷。经典电荷连续，未包含费米子与量子电荷选择，不能直接预测现实宇宙中的量子双荷子丰度。

**English:** Thermal preparation, gauge-covariant UV filtering and a mass quench produce charged monopoles that subsequently lose electric charge. Classical continuous charges and omitted fermions prevent a direct prediction of quantum dyon abundances.
