## 作者英文摘要

We study the covariant Wigner function for the spin-$3/2$ Rarita-Schwinger field and its relation to the spin density matrix and the mean spin polarization vector. We obtain a formula relating the covariant Wigner function to the mean spin vector for a weakly interacting field. We then calculate the mean spin vector induced by thermal vorticity, thermal shear, and spin potential at local thermal equilibrium in the Belinfante, canonical and GLW pseudo-gauges. The resulting formula can be used to predict the spin polarization of spin-$3/2$ hadrons, such as the $\Omega^-$, in heavy-ion collisions. Differences in the thermal-shear induced polarization in the canonical and Belinfante pseudo-gauges make it possible to use local spin polarization measurements of the $\Omega^-$ to discriminate between the two pseudo-gauges.

## 摘要中文翻译

我们研究自旋 3/2 的 Rarita–Schwinger 场的协变 Wigner 函数，以及它与自旋密度矩阵和平均自旋极化矢量的关系。对于弱相互作用场，我们得到把协变 Wigner 函数与平均自旋矢量联系起来的公式。随后，我们在 Belinfante、canonical 和 GLW 伪规范下，计算局域热平衡中由热涡量、热剪切与自旋势诱导的平均自旋矢量。所得公式可用于预测重离子碰撞中 Ω⁻ 等自旋 3/2 强子的自旋极化。canonical 与 Belinfante 伪规范下热剪切诱导极化的差异，使利用 Ω⁻ 的局域自旋极化测量区分这两种伪规范成为可能。

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** Rarita–Schwinger 约束使平均自旋包含额外张量信息。局域热平衡的一阶梯度结果中，canonical 与 Belinfante 的热剪切项不同，可形成实验判别思路；尚未完成具体重离子流体背景预测。

**English:** Rarita–Schwinger constraints leave extra tensor information in the mean spin. First-gradient local-equilibrium expressions differ between canonical and Belinfante thermal-shear contributions, suggesting a discriminator that still requires a concrete hydrodynamic calculation.
