## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** Landau 条件定位候选奇点，Feynman 参数的正性进一步筛选物理支。讲义用积分例子解释连续解析与自举约束，不应把候选奇点条件当作充分存在性证明。

**English:** Landau equations locate candidate singularities, while Feynman-parameter positivity selects the physical sheet. Worked integrals connect analytic continuation to bootstrap constraints; candidate-singularity equations are not sufficient existence proofs.
