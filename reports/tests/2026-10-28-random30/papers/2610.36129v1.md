## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 无 Cauchy 视界时，相对几何项衰减；有潜在内视界时，先放大标量梯度，再发生动能接管。在额外次领阶条件下终点呈标量 Kasner。研究限于给定边界支持的定常小振幅族。

**English:** Without a Cauchy horizon, relative geometric terms decay; near a would-be inner horizon, gradient amplification precedes kinetic dominance. A scalar-Kasner endpoint follows under subleading-term assumptions, within a boundary-supported stationary small-amplitude family.
