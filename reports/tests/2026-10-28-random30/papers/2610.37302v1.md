## 摘要要点转述 / Abstract gist

**中文（转述）：** 纳入反常项的 Noether 分解给出零通量及可积通量下的荷表示定理。

**English (paraphrase):** Noetherian splitting gives charge-representation theorems for vanishing or integrable flux, including anomalies.

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 固定拉氏量及同伦处方后，若通量为 δP，Hamiltonian 荷为 H=Q−P，中心项随之修正。AdS₃ 展示其应用。迁移到你的模型前，仍须明确边界项、场依赖生成元及通量可积性。

**English:** For a specified Lagrangian and homotopy prescription, integrable flux δP leads to H=Q−P and a modified central cocycle. AdS₃ illustrates the construction. Boundary terms, field-dependent generators and flux integrability must be checked before applying it to another model.
