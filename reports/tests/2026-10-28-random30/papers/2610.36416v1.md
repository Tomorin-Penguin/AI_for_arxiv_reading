## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 有限动量宽度、相干相位和自旋纹理进入同一变换核。平均动量与总角动量相同的波包仍可有不同概率中心侧跳；能量加权中心的行为不同。这是运动学参考系效应。

**English:** Momentum width, coherent phases and spin textures enter one finite-boost kernel. Packets with identical mean momentum and total angular momentum can have different probability-centroid shifts; energy-centroid weighting changes the result. The effect is kinematic.
