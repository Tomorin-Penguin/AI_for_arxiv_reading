## 摘要要点转述 / Abstract gist

**中文（转述）：** 探针谱形状因子出现斜坡，并不必然意味着混沌能级关联。

**English (paraphrase):** A ramp in a probe spectral form factor need not imply chaotic level correlations.

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 圆形砖墙的斜坡在谱展开后消失；动量分数化 superstratum 则同时保留斜坡和能级排斥，其间距比约 0.463，介于 Poisson 与 GOE。比较必须固定对称扇区，且这些是探针模而非微观态能谱。

**English:** The circular-brickwall ramp disappears after unfolding. A momentum-fractionated superstratum retains both a ramp and level repulsion, with a spacing ratio near 0.463 between Poisson and GOE. These are symmetry-resolved probe modes, not microscopic-state energies.
