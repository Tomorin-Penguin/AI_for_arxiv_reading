## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 变分目标同时约束潜分布与局域预测。作者要求潜坐标既对应序参量，又改善未观测自旋的预测；仅追踪磁化涨落不够。自旋玻璃示例提供成对副本输入，不能视作毫无结构先验的发现。

**English:** The variational model must both recover an order coordinate and predict unobserved spins. Tracking magnetization fluctuations alone is insufficient. The spin-glass example uses paired replicas, so the input construction supplies relevant structure.
