### 1. 引力中心荷的几何起源

On the geometric origin of central charges in gravity

[2610.37302v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37302v1) · I=5, R=4 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37302v1.md)

**中文：** 固定拉氏量及同伦处方后，若通量为 δP，Hamiltonian 荷为 H=Q−P，中心项随之修正。AdS₃ 展示其应用。迁移到你的模型前，仍须明确边界项、场依赖生成元及通量可积性。

**English:** For a specified Lagrangian and homotopy prescription, integrable flux δP leads to H=Q−P and a modified central cocycle. AdS₃ illustrates the construction. Boundary terms, field-dependent generators and flux integrability must be checked before applying it to another model.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 与你的二维引力边界荷及关系型观测量定义密切相关；密切的是计算框架，不是已经证明新的记忆效应。 Closely relevant to boundary charges and relational observables in your gravity work; it supplies a framework, not a new memory result.

**下一步 / Next:** 以现用二维 dilaton 作用量及边界条件重算 Ω、Q、F，检查 F=δP 是否成立。 Recompute Ω, Q and F for the chosen dilaton action and boundary conditions, then test F=δP.

### 2. 墙中的另一块砖：无视界几何的正常模谱形状因子

Another Brick in the Wall: Spectral Form Factor of Normal Modes in Horizonless Geometries

[2610.37038v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37038v1) · I=5, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37038v1.md)

**中文：** 圆形砖墙的斜坡在谱展开后消失；动量分数化 superstratum 则同时保留斜坡和能级排斥，其间距比约 0.463，介于 Poisson 与 GOE。比较必须固定对称扇区，且这些是探针模而非微观态能谱。

**English:** The circular-brickwall ramp disappears after unfolding. A momentum-fractionated superstratum retains both a ramp and level repulsion, with a spacing ratio near 0.463 between Poisson and GOE. These are symmetry-resolved probe modes, not microscopic-state energies.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 直接帮助审视 SYK/JT 的混沌判据，避免把谱密度效应误判为谱关联。 Useful for assessing SYK/JT chaos diagnostics and separating density effects from spectral correlations.

**下一步 / Next:** 阅读时同时检查谱展开、对称扇区、间距比及复苏时间，不单看斜坡图。 Check unfolding, symmetry sectors, spacing ratios and revival times alongside the ramp.

### 3. AdS₅×S⁵ 与 AdS₄×CP³ 中 AdS₂ 超弦配分函数的两圈修正

On 2-loop AdS$_2$ superstring partition function in AdS$_5 \times S^5$ and AdS$_4\times CP^3$

[2610.37096v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37096v1) · I=5, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37096v1.md)

**中文：** 协变测度处方给出 f₂=0（AdS₅×S⁵）及 3/(32π²)（AdS₄×CP³）；后者可由 M2 高 KK 模抵消。IIB 一侧的缺失贡献仍未解释。这里的 AdS₂ 是弦世界面，不是 JT 时空。

**English:** The covariant measure prescription gives f₂=0 in AdS₅×S⁵ and 3/(32π²) in AdS₄×CP³. Higher M2 KK modes cancel the latter; the missing IIB contribution remains unresolved. AdS₂ here is a string worldsheet, not JT spacetime.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 对 AdS₂ 量子计算、测度和正则化兴趣高度相关，但不直接提供二维引力记忆结果。 Highly relevant to AdS₂ quantum calculations and regularization, without directly establishing a dilaton-gravity memory result.

**下一步 / Next:** 逐项比较作用量顶点与测度接触项，再核对有限系数和局域化预言。 Compare action vertices with measure contact terms before checking finite coefficients against localization.

### 4. The Disordered Universe 2025：非平衡统计系统

The Disordered Universe 2025: Statistical Systems Out of Equilibrium

[2610.36997v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36997v1) · I=5, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.36997v1.md)

**中文：** 由淬火平均、重叠矩阵到一步副本对称性破缺，提供进入无序多体计算的路线。全息部分回顾的 quiver 模型并不具有真正的自旋玻璃相，不能据类比认定 AdS₂ 碎裂就是玻璃化。

**English:** Quenched averaging, overlap matrices and one-step replica breaking provide a route into disordered many-body calculations. The discussed quiver models do not possess a genuine spin-glass phase, so the holographic motivation is not an identification of AdS₂ fragmentation with glassiness.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 最适合补足你学习 SYK 所需的无序平均与大 N 基础。 Especially useful for building the disorder-average and large-N background needed for SYK.

**下一步 / Next:** 先独立完成高斯无序平均，明确平均 log Z 与 log 平均 Z 的差别，再进入双时场。 Derive the Gaussian disorder average and distinguish average log Z from log average Z before bilocal fields.

### 5. 热 OPE 中黑洞信息的复苏结构

Resurgence of Black Holes in Thermal OPE

[2610.37554v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37554v1) · I=4, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37554v1.md)

**中文：** 阶乘增长的高频展开经 Borel 变换连接到 WKB 作用量。完整推迟关联函数只在指定复频楔形区由此重建；区外需要全局 Stokes 数据，不能从局部展开直接宣称重建整个黑洞内部。

**English:** Factorially growing high-frequency coefficients encode WKB actions through Borel singularities. Exact retarded reconstruction holds in a specified complex-frequency wedge; global Stokes data are needed outside it. This does not by itself reconstruct the entire interior.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 适合研究边界数据与体内可观测信息的关系；迁移至 AdS₂ 模型需另算。 Relevant to boundary information and bulk reconstruction; an AdS₂ application requires a separate calculation.

**下一步 / Next:** 先检验平面背景的重建楔 π/2−π/d < arg ω < π/2+π/d，再比较你的边界观测协议。 Check the planar reconstruction wedge before comparing with the proposed boundary-observable protocol.

### 6. 从量子主约束恢复经典规范几何

Recovering classical gauge geometry from quantum master constraints

[2610.37493v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37493v1) · I=4, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37493v1.md)

**中文：** 正规有限维情形下，线性化主流满足 L²=0 时，协方差二阶响应的像给出规范分布。SL(2,R) 示例不是完整引力理论；局域分布不能唯一确定生成元、全局商空间或物理量子扇区。

**English:** In a regular finite-dimensional setting, when L²=0 the image of the covariance's second response recovers gauge directions. The SL(2,R) example does not determine a gravitational constraint algebra, global quotient or unique physical quantum sector.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 对区分规范冗余与物理观测量有方法价值；同为 SL(2,R) 不构成与你项目的物理等价。 Useful for separating gauge redundancy from observables; shared SL(2,R) notation does not establish physical equivalence.

**下一步 / Next:** 先用 m=p²/2 验证协方差剪切，再与 m=(q²+p²)/2 的旋转对照。 Compare covariance shear for m=p²/2 with rotation for m=(q²+p²)/2.

### 7. Kaluza–Klein 约化中的动量空间纠缠与互信息

Momentum-space entanglement and mutual information in Kaluza-Klein reduction

[2610.37009v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37009v1) · I=4, R=3 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.37009v1.md)

**中文：** 五维圆紧致化标量模型中，非导数三次耦合给出单层互信息的 1/n 尾，整座模塔贡献对层数呈对数增长。四次耦合另受硬动量态影响。这里区分的是低能可观测量与约化密度矩阵；并非宣称有效场论失效。

**English:** For a scalar on a circle, nonderivative cubic interactions produce a 1/n mutual-information tail and logarithmic tower accumulation; quartic interactions introduce additional cutoff sensitivity. The distinction concerns low-energy observables versus the complete reduced density matrix, not a failure of effective field theory for its usual observables.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 与你对可观测信息、约化描述及历史数据的兴趣相接；尚未建立与引力记忆的对应。 Useful for thinking about reduced descriptions and accessible information; it does not establish a gravitational-memory mechanism.

**下一步 / Next:** 核对三次相互作用的 1/n 求和及微扰小参数，再考虑有导数的引力耦合是否保留该尾。 Check the perturbative 1/n sum before extending the argument to derivative gravitational couplings.

### 8. Higgs 支上的 BPS 混沌与偶然态：Gepner 点

BPS Chaos and Fortuity in Higgs Branches I: The Gepner Point

[2610.36035v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36035v1) · I=4, R=2 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.36035v1.md)

**中文：** 固定 Kähler 参数时，环境上同调部分的曲率为零；非平凡统计集中于中间上同调。Gepner 点的曲率谱呈 GUE 型间距及谱形状因子，部分分量因反幺正对称性呈 GOE；谱边缘仍有结构性偏离。

**English:** At fixed Kähler parameters the ambient-cohomology curvature vanishes, isolating nontrivial statistics in middle cohomology. At the Gepner point, curvature spectra show GUE-like spacings and form factors, while a component with an antiunitary symmetry is GOE-like. Structured spectral-edge deviations remain.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 与 SYK/RMT 学习强相关，但所研究的是曲率谱，不是 BPS 能量谱。 Strong RMT relevance, with curvature eigenvalues rather than BPS energy eigenvalues as the diagnostic.

**下一步 / Next:** 并列比较曲率谱、探针正常模谱与 SYK 哈密顿量谱，先明确随机矩阵作用于哪个空间。 Compare curvature, probe-mode and SYK Hamiltonian spectra while identifying the space on which each operator acts.

### 9. 长程 Ising 链中的介子、禁闭与 Krylov 复杂度

Mesons, Confinement, and Complexity: A Krylov Space Perspective on the Long-Range Ising Chain

[2610.36054v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36054v1) · I=4, R=2 · [阅读卡](papers/2610.36054v1.md)

**中文：** 弱淬火时，态复杂度的平均值约为 2Lh_x²/m₁²，振荡频率追踪最低介子质量。数值时间窗排除了更慢的宇称隧穿，不能视作严格无限时极限；热算符内积也会改变所见谱权重。

**English:** After a weak quench, mean state complexity scales approximately as 2Lh_x²/m₁² and oscillates at the lightest meson frequency. The finite averaging window excludes slower parity tunnelling, while thermal operator inner products reweight the spectrum.

**推荐理由 / Why read:** 可为 SYK 学习提供可计算对照；复杂度振荡不等于混沌证据。 A calculable comparison for SYK studies; complexity oscillations alone are not evidence of chaos.

**下一步 / Next:** 先在小链上复算弱淬火标度，并区分态复杂度和算符复杂度。 Reproduce the weak-quench scaling on a small chain and distinguish state from operator complexity.
