# 共形反常的 Wess–Zumino 作用量 / The Wess-Zumino Action of the Conformal Anomaly

**arXiv:** [2610.36735v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36735v1) · **作者:** Emil Mottola · hep-th new / gr-qc cross

## English abstract key-point paraphrase

A local Wess–Zumino treatment of the conformal anomaly in two and four spacetime dimensions introduces a scalar conformalon as an effective infrared degree of freedom. The WZ-consistent action, its scalar variational principle and stress tensor encode anomalous Weyl Ward identities; in two dimensions the construction localizes Polyakov's nonlocal anomaly action. The paper further argues that anomaly poles and their spectral sum rules have an infrared origin, and examines the seemingly cohomologically trivial $\Box R$ term in four dimensions.

## 中文摘要转述

作者给出 $D=2,4$ 共形反常的局域 Wess–Zumino 有效作用量及应力张量，以标量 conformalon $\varphi$ 描述 Weyl 变换下的反常结构。在二维，作用量与 Polyakov 非局域作用量的局域化形式等价。作者讨论 $\langle TT\rangle$ 关联函数的无质量 pole、光锥传播子及 UV 有限谱和规则，主张反常有效动力学的重要内容在红外而非单纯依赖 UV 正则化。

## 独立正文阅读概括 / Independent reading synthesis

**中文：** §2.1 从 $\langle T^\mu{}_\mu\rangle=(N/24\pi)R$ (2) 出发。§2.2 将 $\langle TT\rangle$ 谱函数在无质量极限的 $\delta(s)$ (29) 与 anomaly pole 对应；这是一项作者给出的由 QFT 到低能标量的解释。§2.3 将 Weyl 轨道参数 $\varphi$ 代入 WZ 插值积分，得
$$W_{2D}[g;\varphi]=\frac{N}{96\pi}\int d^2x\sqrt{-g}\,[\varphi\Box\varphi+2R\varphi],$$
式 (30)，约定与边界分部积分项均以本文为准。其作用量对 $\varphi$ 的变分给出与异常消失的代表度规相关的方程；对 $g$ 变分再构造 SET。§2.4 用 canonical $[\varphi,\Pi_\varphi]$ 与 $\langle TT\rangle$ 的 Schwinger term 配对（(38)–(41)），§2.5 检验 WZ 的 cocycle 条件 (45)；**作者要求相应边界条件使 $\Box$ 为 Hermitian**，不能只依据无边界的局域公式推断边界态。§3 在四维引入 Paneitz operator $\Delta_4$，完整 WZ 作用量含 $\Box R$ 的独立贡献 (74)–(90)。作者认为 WZ 变分原则减少选择背景 conformal frame 时的歧义；该强结论属于特定有效场论处方，需要与其他量子有效作用量分解的自由度区别。

**English:** The key transferable structure is the Wess–Zumino interpolation along a Weyl orbit rather than simply integrating a trace anomaly in an arbitrarily split metric $g=e^{2\sigma}\bar g$. In D=2 it yields the local scalar form $W\propto\int\sqrt{-g}(\varphi\Box\varphi+2R\varphi)$ of the Polyakov anomaly action. Variation produces a stress tensor with the correct anomalous trace on shell; the canonical scalar commutator reproduces the stress-tensor Schwinger term. The WZ cocycle condition requires attention to Hermiticity and boundary conditions. In D=4 the general action involves $\Delta_4$ and a contribution from $\Box R$, treated by the author as infrared-relevant. The “conformalon is a physical propagating Nambu–Goldstone field” is the author's interpretation, not an independent proof that every two-dimensional dilaton gravitational model acquires an additional physical edge mode.

## 与当前二维 dilaton / Liouville 研究的关系
