Using boundary-supported stationary black-hole families, the author compares how small scalar input evolves into scalar-kinetic dominance inside Schwarzschild-like and Kerr–Newman-like horizons. The area contraction drives both transitions; in the latter a sharp increase in the scalar gradient near the would-be Cauchy horizon yields a nonlinear positive minimum of a metric diagnostic.

## 独立正文概括 / Independent body synthesis
**中文：** 关键不是宣称所有内视界都会消失，而是先建立在给定解族可计算的诊断变量。横截面积收缩定义为 \(\rho=-\tfrac12\ln(dA/dA_h)\)（式 (3)），径向诊断 \(\mathcal F=-r_h^2[\nabla(\rho-\rho_i)]^2\)（式 (4)）。Route I 表明，尽管标量梯度保持小量，面积收缩也能令相对曲率贡献减弱，标量动能占优；Route II 则在未反应的 Kerr–Newman 内视界根附近放大梯度，使 \(\widetilde{\mathcal F}_{\min}>0\)（式 (13)）。晚期近似使用 \(\beta=\lim\dot\Psi\) 和剪切 \(\gamma\)，Kasner 指数（式 (19)）满足 \(\sum p_j=1,\ \sum p_j^2+2p_\Psi^2=1\)。这些是 4D Einstein–Maxwell–标量的定常边界支撑解族及其**局部内部**结论，不能据此直接断言任意塌缩时空的全局视界拓扑。

**English:** The common physical variable is logarithmic contraction of transverse area. In the Schwarzschild-like route, contraction suppresses the relative curvature term without a large scalar derivative; in the Kerr–Newman route, approach to the would-be inner-horizon zero amplifies the scalar gradient until a positive nonlinear minimum replaces that zero. An asymptotic scalar–Kasner regime satisfies the modified Kasner constraints. The result is conditional on the stationary boundary-driven Einstein–Maxwell–scalar setup and does not establish a general global-causal-structure theorem.
