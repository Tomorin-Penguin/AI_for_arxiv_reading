## English abstract key-point paraphrase

The paper develops Majorana-trace combinatorics for a double-scaled, Maldacena–Qi-coupled SYK setup that admits an AS² baby-universe saddle. A heavy operator can drive a first-order competition between a thermal-AdS saddle and a closed-universe saddle, even in a low-temperature limit. The authors propose an interior relational-time evolution that produces a cosine-shaped spatial size.

## 中文摘要要点转述

在具有 Maldacena–Qi 耦合的双标度 SYK 模型中，作者以 Majorana 算符迹及弦图规则分析 AS² 型闭合婴儿宇宙。重算符插入与耦合比值控制热 AdS 和婴儿宇宙 saddle 的一阶相变；用与婴儿宇宙内部重粒子相关的时间演化，可以得到随时间按余弦变化的空间尺度。

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 文章的关键区分是边界时间与内部关系型时间。§3 通过对带重算符的两点迹 $Z_{AS^2}(\beta)$ 进行 Dyson 展开及 Majorana 指标组合求和，构造 wormhole chord 数的 saddle；在 §3.1 的近似下出现竞争的 $m_*$：一个在 $\Delta$ 附近，另一个约 $4\mathcal J/(\lambda\mu)$（式 3.23–3.25 与图 3）。有效 crossing 参数为 $\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}=\lambda[1-(\Delta/m)^2]$（式 3.17），**其推导使用 chord 组成分配等近似，不能说整套 wormhole 求和在任意参数下严格**。在 §4，简单地随外部 $H_{MQ}$ 演化并不会改变内部 chord 几何；作者转而让重算符以 $H_{\rm free}$ 定义的内部时间演化（式 4.4–4.6），使 $\mathcal J/\mu$ 的有效值乘以 $|\cos(\mu t)|$，于是 $m_*^{AS^2}(t)=4\mathcal J|\cos(\mu t)|/(\lambda\mu)$（式 4.8）。作者将其解释为 big bang/big crunch 几何，并强调婴儿宇宙的全局 Hilbert space 等结构仍未完备建立（§5.1）。

**English:** The main distinction is between boundary evolution and evolution relative to an object inside a closed universe. Majorana combinatorics and a saddle approximation to the heavy-operator correlator yield competing chord-number configurations: one near the insertion mass and another of order $4\mathcal J/(\lambda\mu)$. The effective chord-crossing penalty depends on $\lambda_{\rm eff}$, but the wormhole-saddle estimates rely on explicit large-parameter and distributional approximations. Ordinary boundary evolution leaves the interior chord structure unaffected. A proposed clock based on evolution of the heavy operator by the MQ free Hamiltonian produces $m_*^{AS^2}(t)\propto |\cos(\mu t)|$. The authors interpret this as a closed AdS2 big-bang/big-crunch patch; the interior clock choice and the fully global quantum geometry are not independently established universal observables.
