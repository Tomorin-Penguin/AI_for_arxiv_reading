# 5PM 黑洞散射中的 K3 Landau 奇点和记忆积分 / Landau singularity of the K3 maximal cut in black-hole scattering at fifth post-Minkowskian order

**arXiv:** [2610.36128v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36128v1) · **作者:** Matthew D. Schwartz · hep-th new  
**正文:** https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36128v1 · **许可:** CC BY 4.0  
**状态:** body_reviewed；阅读 §1、§2.1–2.3、§3.1–3.5、§4–5 方法、§6.1–6.4、§7（54页；未进行完整四圈计算）。**最终 I=5, R=4**。

## English abstract key-point paraphrase

At fifth post-Minkowskian order the maximal cut of a K3-sector integral develops an actual pinch of its integration torus when the relative Lorentz factor is $\gamma=3$. This explains the Landau singularity underlying a divergence that must cancel between regions in physical scattering. The paper also derives boundary integrals of the gravitational memory region, explores analytic continuation of K3 and Calabi–Yau periods, and clarifies that an input retarded-propagator routing remains necessary.

## 中文摘要转述

五阶后闵氏引力两体散射中的四圈积分涉及 K3/Calabi–Yau 几何。作者证明洛伦兹因子 $\gamma=3$ 处的 K3 maximal cut 穿过积分轮廓、成为 Landau pinch；该单项的发散不应被当成物理散射奇点，必须由不同贡献抵消。文章另计算引力记忆区积分的若干边界系数、研究束缚轨道的解析延拓；retarded propagator 的 routing 依然是非平凡选择。

## 独立阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** §1 细分 potential 与 radiation/memory 区贡献，特别提醒散射“memory region”源于永久位移的非线性记忆，但本文**没有直接从波形计算记忆跃变**。§2 引入 $\gamma=(1+x^2)/(2x)$（式 1）和 worldline EFT / 多圈积分。§3 把 Baikov 的切割曲面写成 $Q_6=P_+P_-$（式 13），发现积分环面上 $w=4\cos(\phi_1/2)\cos((\phi_1-2\phi_2)/2)$；在 $\gamma=3$ 时 $P_-=0$ 的节点落到 $t_1=t_2=1$ 处，积分 contour 被 pinch（式 14、§3.2）。这个几何判断比仅见 Picard–Fuchs 微分方程有奇点更强。§6 的 memory boundary integrals $I^{(M)}_1,I^{(M)}_2,I^{(M)}_3$ 由作者在预先指定 retarded routing 后求值，从而得到 $c_M=1$，**但 Landau pinch 不能自行固定 routing**；作者明确说没有由第一原理推导该 routing，也没有给出完整的 5PM observable。

**English:** The paper identifies the analytic origin of a 5PM divergence using geometry of the maximal cut, not by fully reducing the four-loop observable. The K3 cut surface has a nodal point on the physical integration torus at $\gamma=3$, producing a second-type Landau pinch. A physical observable must cancel the spurious singularity, with the non-linear gravitational-wave memory region contributing the necessary boundary integrals. The author evaluates these integrals under a chosen retarded-graviton routing, recovering $c_M=1$, but explicitly does not derive a unique routing from the Landau locus or compute a complete observable. The heavy K3 analysis is not equivalent to a derivation of the permanent memory displacement in simple asymptotically flat gravity.
