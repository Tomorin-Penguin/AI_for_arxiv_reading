## English abstract key-point paraphrase

At leading large N, two-dimensional QCD with fundamental quarks is treated on rigid AdS2 and dS2. A null gauge and affine parametrization preserve the simple Coulomb sector, while the spacetime SL(2,R) Casimir supplements the flat meson equation by a curvature-sensitive Legendre operator. The work studies spectra, boundary-flux conditions, integrable structure and the fate of confined mesons in expanding de Sitter space.

## 独立正文阅读概括 / Independent synthesis

**中文：** 把几何信息尽量隔离在运动学生成元，是全文最可迁移的思想。§3 取 null gauge $A_-=0$ 和仿射 $\lambda$，使规范作用量在 $(y,\lambda)$ 具有平直形式（式 3.7）；§4 构造 $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ Casimir，得到 $\mathcal C_2=R^2\hat M^2_{\rm flat}+\mathcal C_{\rm coll}$（式 4.12）。对两体内部变量 $x$，$\mathcal C_{\rm coll}=-\partial_x[x(1-x)\partial_x]$（式 5.3），所以介子维数解满足作者的积分-微分本征方程 (5.4)，其左侧为 $\Delta(\Delta-1)\varphi$。在相应端点条件下，式 (5.7) 的正定二次型给出 $\Delta\geq1$。§7 区分边界 gauge flux 的 Neumann 与 Dirichlet 类型以及超选扇区，§9 在 dS₂ 得到 Casimir 中 $R^2\to -R^2$ 的代数继续，但 **AdS₂ 的谱正定性与真空证明不会一起解析继续**；dS₂ 中的 meson resonance 还依赖外向边界条件与谱的定义。作者在 §10 给出 AdS“压缩”、边界牵引和 dS“撕裂”三种有效图像。

**English:** The transferable result is an algebraic separation of geometry from interactions. An affine null coordinate keeps the non-Abelian gauge Coulomb term identical to that in flat QCD2. The AdS2 quadratic Casimir splits into a flat invariant-mass operator and a kinematic collinear Casimir; in the two-body sector the latter becomes the Legendre differential operator. Thus boundary dimensions follow from a curved-space ’t Hooft integral equation, subject to endpoint and flux boundary conditions. In dS2 the sign of the geometric term reverses, but positivity, vacuum structure and normalizability arguments do not simply continue from AdS. Mesons become resonances whose definition uses an outgoing-wave prescription. These are statements about a fixed geometry and the planar limit, not a derivation of quantum dilaton-gravity boundary dynamics.
