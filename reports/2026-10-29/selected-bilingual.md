### 1. [On the geometric origin of central charges in gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37302v1) — I5 R5
**中文：** 以 Noetherian charge/flux 分解规范 Hamiltonian 的定义。当 $F_\xi=\delta P_\xi$ 时使用 $H_\xi=Q_\xi-P_\xi$；中心 2-cocycle 因而含通量修正（§2.3, (47)–(52)）。可对照你的 2D dilaton 边界荷及场依赖残余重参数变换。**限制：**论文 AdS₃/PBH 例子并不自动证明 AdS₂ 结论。  
**English:** A Lagrangian-based Noetherian split identifies the Hamiltonian charge and central cocycle for vanishing or integrable flux. For exact flux $F_\xi=\delta P_\xi$, the physical generator is $H_\xi=Q_\xi-P_\xi$. Apply the procedure to field-dependent AdS2 boundary symmetries only after independently checking boundary conditions; the authors' illustrative derivation is for AdS3.  
**推荐段落:** §2.2–2.3, §3.3–3.4. **可验算:** 在既有二维模型中比较 $\delta Q-F$ 的可积性。 [完整阅读卡](papers/2610.37302v1.md)。

### 2. [Cosmology of the Baby Universe in Coupled SYK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36032v1) — I5 R4
**中文：** 对耦合 SYK 生成的闭合婴儿宇宙，普通边界时间演化不改变内部 chord 几何；重粒子定义的内部时间给出 $m_*(t)\propto |\cos\mu t|$（§4, (4.4)–(4.8)）。可借此研究“时钟定义和观测协议先于几何记忆判定”的问题。**限制：**内部时间处方为作者提出的特定操作，wormhole saddle 的组合估计含近似。  
**English:** The heavy-insertion saddle of coupled double-scaled SYK exhibits a competing baby-universe phase. Boundary evolution leaves the interior chord number unchanged, while the authors' proposed internal clock yields a size proportional to $|\cos(\mu t)|$. The clock construction is model-specific and the saddle estimates rely on approximations; it is a comparison point, not a proof of universal gravitational memory.  
**推荐段落:** §3.1, §4–5. **可验算:** 比较 $H_{MQ}$ 时间与 $H_{\rm free}$ 内部时间。 [完整阅读卡](papers/2610.36032v1.md)。

### 3. [The 't Hooft model on (A)dS₂](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37667v1) — I5 R4
**中文：** 利用仿射 null gauge 和 $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ Casimir，得到 $\mathcal C_2=R^2\hat M^2_{\rm flat}+\mathcal C_{\rm coll}$，内部波函数上 $\mathcal C_{\rm coll}=-\partial_x[x(1-x)\partial_x]$（(4.12),(5.3)）；边界 gauge flux 与 dS₂ 共振还取决于边界条件。**限制：**固定背景 QCD₂，不是 dynamical JT。  
**English:** Null-gauge quantization keeps the planar Coulomb interaction flat-space-like, while the spacetime Casimir adds a Legendre operator to the meson equation. Boundary flux and de Sitter resonances show the importance of endpoint conditions. This is QCD2 on a rigid background, not a JT-gravity reduction.  
**推荐段落:** §3–5, §7, §9. **可验算:** 从 $\mathcal C_2$ 导出 (5.4)。[完整阅读卡](papers/2610.37667v1.md)。
